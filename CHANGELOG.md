# Changelog

## 2.0.1

- **Welcome guide:** a short animated tour on first launch shows where Ordock lives and how to use it,
  with the language choice (reopen it anytime from the menu)
- **macOS installer:** `Ordock.dmg` with the classic window, just drag Ordock onto Applications
- **New icon:** the Ordock penguin, with the rainbow Dock on its belly
- **Matching menu bar icon** in black and white, that adapts to light and dark mode
- Ordock can no longer be opened twice: opening it again shows the settings window
- If macOS doesn't save the new order, the Dock is left untouched and you get a message
  (before, the Dock restarted anyway)
- Pressing the shortcut several times in a row no longer restarts the Dock again and again
- Restore never writes anything if the Dock can't be read
- Empty saved orders can no longer be created
- New built-in categories added in future versions also appear for people who reordered their groups

## 2.0.0

**Riordina Dock is now Ordock**, with a new icon and many new ways to tidy up your Dock.

- **Sort by last used:** most recent apps on the left or on the right of the Dock
- **Sort by category:** apps grouped (Browsing, Mail, Socials, Design, Video, Music & audio, Work,
  Development, Utilities, Fun, System, Other) with a small space between groups
  - click a category to put it first in the Dock
  - automatic recognition of common apps, custom groups, alphabetical or custom group order
- **Saved orders:** save named Dock layouts and apply them with one click
- **Dock clean-up:** find apps unused for 1, 3, 6 or 12 months, or deleted from the Mac
- **Keyboard shortcut:** ⌃⌥⌘ O repeats the last sort from any app
- **Settings window** with every option in one place
- **Language choice:** English or Italian, while installing and anytime from the menu
- Modern menu with system symbols
- Ready-to-use app for Apple Silicon and Intel Macs attached to every release
- Backups and settings of Riordina Dock are carried over automatically

## 1.0.0

First version, as **Riordina Dock**.

- Sort the Dock by name or by color
- Restore the original order with one click
- English and Italian interface
